QUESTIONS

The fine print, in plain words.

How can it be free?

Transcribing, picking moments and rendering all happen on your computer or phone. Nothing is uploaded, so there's no per-video cost for us to cover. Support pays for development time, not servers.

What does a supporter get that free users don't?

A badge, and the knowledge that you're paying for the next improvement. We don't hold features back for paying users.

Do I need an account?

Not to clip. You need a free account only to support Clip Machine (so we know who to show the badge to) or to sync your presets between devices.

How do I cancel?

Open the account menu in the workspace and choose Manage billing. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you stay a supporter until the end of the period you paid for.

Who handles my card?

Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe and never touch Clip Machine's server. We keep only Stripe's customer and subscription ids and whether a payment went through. See the privacy page.

Why yearly and monthly?

Yearly costs the same as 10 months, so it's cheaper per month, and it saves a renewal fee on each payment. Monthly is there if you'd rather not commit.
